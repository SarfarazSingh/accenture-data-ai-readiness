Data & AI architecture readiness

The diagnostic I would run in the first two weeks of a data and AI strategy engagement: score eight capabilities, see the gap on the client's reference architecture, find out which AI use cases those gaps are blocking, and get a sequenced roadmap. Move any score and everything below recalculates.

Where this fits in Technology Strategy & Advisory

A diagnostic that becomes the proposal

Scores, blocked use cases and the roadmap come out of one workshop. The roadmap horizons map straight onto a phased statement of work.

Architecture tied to value

Clients fund platforms when they see which use cases those platforms unlock. The tool makes that link explicit, use case by use case.

Built by someone who has shipped it

At PwC I shipped a GenAI assistant to 2M+ users and rebuilt a contact-centre architecture for 40% more capacity. Before that, ten years running technical operations on submarines.
